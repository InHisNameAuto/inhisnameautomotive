Guaranteed Workmanship

PREMIUM COMPONENTS.
BACKED FOR 12 MONTHS OR 12,000 MILES.

A warranty is only as reliable as the components installed under the hood. We refuse to cut corners with cheaply made retail box-store parts that are prone to early failure. Instead, we source premium, professional-grade components and Original Equipment (OE) matching parts directly through top-tier commercial distribution networks like FMP (Factory Motor Parts), Worldpac, and Parts Authority (IMC / Fast Undercar). Because we invest exclusively in commercial-tier quality, we proudly back our workmanship with a written 12-Month / 12,000-Mile warranty.
